!! Letter to God !!

The ideology of the organization is that the needs of all living creatures of the world are being fulfilled by the almighty god. As per the above ideology, all requirements of Prabhuji of Ashrams are endorsed to the Thakur ji by writing a letter named as “Thakur Ji Ki Chitthi (Letter to God)” on the board fixed at every Ashrams. In this letter to God, Ashram’s requirements be it needle or food items, medicine and related items, land, building, clothing, service staff, doctors, professionals and even the need of last rituals of Prabhuji are submitted to the Thakur ji.
