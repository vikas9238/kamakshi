Kamdhenu Gaushala

Cows are worshipped in hindu culture as it is regarded as an abode of Hindu Gods and Goddesses. It is believed that one can attain liberation by simply serving a cow. Our scriptures praise the glory of cow as not only as sacred but also an auspicious of all being.
